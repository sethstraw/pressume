# Code of conduct

pressume should be a welcoming place to learn, contribute, and disagree well.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while taking
responsibility for impact. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in
project spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior undermines a safe, productive community. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation is not tolerated.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
